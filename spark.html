Service and experience transformation

SHiFT Spark is a structured transformation engagement designed for organisations ready to rethink, redesign or strengthen how their services and experiences work in practice.

Book a discovery call → Start with a Catalyst first
Sound familiar?

You already know something needs to shift.

You can see the friction your participants, clients or staff are experiencing. You can feel the operational strain sitting underneath the day-to-day. Teams are compensating for systems that no longer quite fit, services have evolved over time without a deliberate redesign, and important experiences are being shaped by circumstance instead of intention.

Most organisations don't lack commitment or care. They lack the time, structure and internal capacity to step back and redesign the experience properly.

So the work stays on the wishlist while another quarter passes, another operational workaround becomes permanent, and another participant or staff member quietly disengages.

SHiFT Spark creates the space, structure and strategic support to redesign those experiences properly; collaboratively, practically and with implementation in mind from the beginning.

What SHiFT Spark is

A 16–20 week strategic transformation engagement.

Together, we explore how the experience currently works, identify where friction and opportunities exist, and co-design a future state that is grounded in both human experience and operational reality.

Some organisations arrive knowing exactly what needs redesigning. Others use Catalyst first to identify where the greatest opportunity exists.

The engagement

A structured process from clarity through to implementation.

The timeline flexes depending on organisational complexity, stakeholder involvement and whether participant research is included. The work doesn't stop at handover; it carries 90 days of support beyond it.

Week 1
Prepare
Project setup, stakeholder planning, existing material review and engagement preparation tailored to your organisation.
Week 2
Align
Leadership conversations and strategic alignment sessions to clarify goals, context, priorities and success measures.
Weeks 3–6
Discovery
Research with staff, stakeholders and, where appropriate, participants or customers to understand the experience as it actually exists in practice.
Weeks 7–8
Synthesis
Insights, patterns, friction points and opportunities synthesised into a clear understanding of the current state and areas for transformation.
Weeks 9–10
Co-design
Collaborative workshops and working sessions to design future-state experiences, operational approaches and service improvements together.
Weeks 11–12
Final design
Development of future-state artefacts, implementation priorities, roadmap recommendations and operational guidance.
Weeks 13–14
Handover
Capability transfer, team alignment, implementation preparation and transition into the 90-day support period.

Then: 90 days of implementation stabilisation support

Fortnightly check-ins, strategic guidance and implementation support to help maintain momentum and embed the work sustainably. You're not implementing alone.

Deliverables

What you walk away with

Every Spark engagement produces a tailored set of strategic and operational artefacts designed to support implementation, alignment and sustainable change. Depending on scope, outputs may include:

01

Current-state journey or ecosystem maps

A clear picture of how the service or experience actually functions in practice, with friction, gaps and opportunities made visible.

02

Participant, customer or staff experience insights

Research synthesis that captures how people are actually experiencing your services; including what's working and where the friction lives.

03

Future-state service and experience designs

Co-designed future-state experiences built collaboratively with your team, grounded in operational realities and human-centred principles.

04

Operational and service blueprints

Roles, systems, handoffs, touchpoints and metrics documented in a format your operations team can work from immediately.

05

Strategic recommendations and transformation roadmap

Prioritised opportunities and a practical Now / Next / Later implementation pathway with ownership recommendations and momentum actions.

06

Capability transfer and team guidance

Knowledge, tools and frameworks transferred to your team so the work continues beyond the engagement; not locked away in a consultant's system.

Collaborative co-design sessions Leadership and implementation support 90 days of stabilisation and follow-up guidance Optional ethical AI opportunity mapping
The process

How it unfolds.

Four steps from discovery conversation to ongoing implementation support.

01

Discovery conversation

We begin with a strategic conversation to understand your organisation, what challenges or opportunities are emerging, and whether Spark is the right fit. If it is, we define the scope, priorities and engagement approach together.

02

Understanding the current state

Through discovery, stakeholder engagement and experience mapping, we build a clear understanding of how the service or experience currently functions in practice.

03

Designing the future state

Working collaboratively with your team, we develop future-state experiences, operational approaches and implementation priorities grounded in your organisational realities.

04

Supporting implementation

The engagement concludes with handover, capability transfer and a structured support period designed to help the work continue beyond the project itself.

Why me

The lens I bring to your work.

I trained as an industrial designer in Buenos Aires, but the longer I worked inside organisations, the more I realised the real challenge was rarely the product itself. It was the experience around it.

The systems people had to navigate. The invisible friction teams compensated for every day. The gap between what organisations intended and what people actually experienced.

That shift led me from designing products into designing services, experiences and organisational systems. My work sits at the intersection of strategy, service design and implementation; working closely with organisations delivering complex human-centred services, particularly in community, social impact and regulated environments.

The goal is never transformation for transformation's sake. It is creating services and systems that better support the people delivering them and the people relying on them.

That’s the lens I bring to your work.
