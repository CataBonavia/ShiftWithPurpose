Find your fit

Pick your Spark

Three redesigns, each scoped to one critical experience. If yours doesn't fit any of these, the discovery call is where we shape a fourth.

The Onboarding Spark

The first 30 days, redesigned end-to-end.

From enquiry to settled; where the experience either compounds or quietly leaks.

Best for

Disability services, aged care providers, NDIS providers, and community service organisations losing participants in the early days; or seeing inconsistent first impressions across the team.

What's redesigned

Enquiry, intake, welcome, first service, first milestone; and the handoffs between them.

The Retention Spark

The moments that decide whether people stay, leave, or refer.

Designed deliberately, instead of left to chance.

Best for

Organisations with healthy intake but quiet attrition; or those that depend on referrals and renewals for sustainable growth, where the experience needs to compound to keep working.

What's redesigned

Ongoing engagement, milestone moments, feedback loops, renewal and referral touchpoints.

The Team Spark

Your team is the experience; their first 90 days redesigned.

From offer letter to the day they can hold the work without you in the room.

Best for

Organisations growing or hiring frequently into frontline roles; where induction quality drives service quality, and where staff turnover in the first six months is the trend nobody wants to name.

What's redesigned

Offer-to-day-one, induction, buddy systems, capability check-ins, the first 90-day review.

Why this works

Why this shift has a ripple effect.

There are many reasons why this approach works. Here are three that matter most.

It's deliberate.

We redesign one experience end-to-end, so the changes connect rather than compete. Most CX work fails because the pieces sit alongside each other instead of compounding. We design for coherence.

It's co-designed with your team.

Your people build it, so they own it. That's why implementation actually lands here when it usually doesn't. The changes hold because your people designed them; not because someone told them to.

It's designed for your tech.

We focus on what you can deliver with your existing tech, unless you're planning an upgrade. We also bring an AI lens to amplify your team; applied deliberately, ethics-first. AI should amplify purpose, not replace the people behind it.

The engagement

Eight weeks of strategic design. Then 90 days of implementation support.

The work doesn't disappear at handover. It comes to life.

Week 1
Prepare
We align diaries, refine stakeholder engagement, and gather data. This is critical for the success of the weeks ahead.
Week 2
Align
One-on-one with you and up to two more leaders; plus a two-hour strategic workshop with your key players to understand the context, the direction, and the real needs.
Week 3
Discovery
Research with your team and, where appropriate, your participants. We learn what's actually happening; not what's documented. Sector-aware, ethics-first.
Week 4
Synthesis and current-state mapping
The experience as it actually exists, mapped end-to-end. Where loyalty leaks, where the team is quietly compensating, where it's almost working but not quite landing.
Week 5
Co-design session one: future-state vision
Two hours with your team. We design the experience your participants deserve; anchored in your values and built within your real constraints.
Week 6
Co-design session two: service blueprint and AI mapping
Two hours with your team. We translate the vision into a service blueprint with roles, systems, and touchpoints; and the AI Opportunity Map alongside it.
Week 7
Final design and implementation planning
All deliverables built. The 90-day implementation rollout plan, with named owners and metrics.
Week 8
Handover and team training
A working session with your team to transfer the design, the blueprint, and the rollout plan. The 90-day support window opens here.

Then: 90 days of implementation support

Fortnightly 30-minute check-ins so the design doesn't stall when it hits the real world. You're not implementing alone.
