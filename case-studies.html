Organisation

Sector

Community development start-up

Type of work

Designing innovative housing solutions for low income people in Argentina

Human-centred design turned a three-month bathroom build into eight hours. In low-income Buenos Aires suburbs, children who had been missing school started attending consistently — because the fundamental infrastructure of their daily lives was finally working.

“Her problem solving and out-of-the-box thinking helped us create a product that had never been created before — even when everyone else said it was impossible.”
