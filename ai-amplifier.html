Why this matters

You already know AI could help. You just don't have time to figure it out.

The marketing manager in most disability services or aged care organisations is doing the work of three people. Commercial marketing. Participant attraction. Internal communications. Compliance comms, sector reputation; the list goes on.

Meanwhile, the AI tools everyone is talking about are designed for tech companies, not for organisations trying to explain a complex funding model to families who are frightened and overwhelmed.

And the question hanging over everything: if we adopt AI, are we replacing the empathy and the relationships that define what we do? The AI Amplifier exists to answer that.

The process

How the AI Impact Amplifier works

Step 1

Audit your current workflows

We map what your team is actually doing: what takes the most time, what requires the most cognitive effort, what gets deprioritised because there is not enough of either. This is the baseline everything else is built from.

Step 2

Identify the amplification opportunities

We start by asking: if we could hire anyone to help this team, what would that person do? Using the Think Then AI framework, we work through your workflows together and ask what can be eliminated, simplified, automated, or delegated to AI; always specific to your context, your values, and your team's capacity.

Step 3

Build the tools together

We do not hand you a technology recommendation and leave. We build the tools with you; prompts, workflows, templates, systems; inside the platforms you already use or are willing to adopt. We are platform agnostic and will prepare you for platform changes as they come.

Step 4

Train your team to use them independently

The goal is capability, not dependency. By the end of the engagement, your team can adapt, extend, and own their AI tools without needing ongoing support. They will feel like they have superpowers.

Our commitment

Built around your values, not the technology vendor's

AI in purpose-led organisations requires a different lens. The people we serve are often vulnerable. The work we do is often confidential. The trust our organisations hold is not a marketing asset; it is the foundation of everything.

The AI Impact Amplifier uses the Think Then AI methodology; a human-centred framework developed by us in collaboration with Sofía Geyer from The Human Lab (Argentina). The framework starts with understanding your people and processes before any technology is introduced.

We never automate without asking whether we should. We never adopt a tool without understanding who carries the risk if it does not work as expected.

Our non-negotiables

  • AI should amplify your people's capacity; it should not replace the judgment, the empathy, or the relationships that define purpose-led work
  • We never recommend a tool we have not tested ourselves in purpose-led contexts
  • Your team's data, your participants' data, and your organisation's IP are always protected
  • Platform agnostic; we work with what you have or help you choose what fits
  • Ethics-first is not a box to tick. It is the starting point for every decision we make together
