What’s inside the template

The seven stages of the client journey

The template walks you through every stage of the relationship; not just the service delivery part, but everything before and after it too.

0

Trigger

Before they know you exist. What prompts someone to start looking for what you offer?

1

Research

When they’re looking for answers, not yet for you. Where are they looking, and what do they find?

2

Decide

The moment of commitment. What does it feel like to say yes to you?

3

Join

They said yes. Does what happens next feel like the beginning of something good?

4

Engage

The long middle. Does every touchpoint add value, or exist because it always has?

5

Grow

The client isn’t the same person who joined. Is your experience designing for who they are now?

6

Go On

They’ve finished, paused, or left. How you handle this shapes whether they come back and what story they tell.
