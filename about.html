What we work on

Three areas. One integrated practice.

Every engagement draws on all three. The entry point varies. The outcome is always the same: an experience that works end-to-end.

01

Customer & Employee Experience

How it feels to be a participant, client, or staff member from first contact to ongoing relationship. We design it deliberately, not by accident.

02

Service Design

The end-to-end architecture of how your organisation delivers. The processes, the touchpoints, the decisions, and the systems that hold the experience together.

03

Implementation Support

We stay alongside you until the work is embedded. Not consulting from a distance — designing the detail, supporting change, and building capability so the organisation runs it independently.

Catalina Bonavia

Strategic and hands-on service designer.

Catalina is an Argentine-born industrial designer and service strategist based in Melbourne. She has spent her career at the intersection of human experience and systems change — from designing a bathroom that could be built in eight hours by construction workers with no prior knowledge, to building the Employee Experience Practice at RMIT University.

She founded SHiFT with Purpose because she kept watching the same pattern repeat: purpose-led organisations doing genuinely important work, with good people and real intent, whose participant and employee experience was being held back by systems, sequences, and structures that were never designed for them.

She works directly with C-level leaders and founders in disability services, aged care, community services, and social enterprise. She does not send junior consultants. Every engagement is delivered by Cata, from the first workshop to the final handover — because continuity of thinking is part of the design.

Moving countries teaches you to read unfamiliar environments quickly. To notice what others have stopped seeing. To ask the question the room has decided is too obvious, or too uncomfortable. That's the lens every engagement starts with.

How we work

The SHiFT Method

Four stages. One continuous thread. Everything connects.

SEE

Map the experience as it actually exists

Not the org chart version. We sit with frontline workers, interview participants and clients, and collaborate with leadership to build a picture that brings both worlds together. We ask the questions the room has stopped asking.

SHAPE

Design around behaviours and needs

Co-design workshops where your team builds the solution with us. We design for how humans actually behave; then layer in AI and other technology deliberately. The change is easier because your people designed it.

SHiFT

Implement so it doesn't stay on a slide deck

Change activity, communications, and hands-on support through implementation. We stay alongside you — not watching from the sideline, but making sure the design becomes the reality.

SUSTAIN

Build capability so it keeps improving

Track what changes, measure what matters, and build the internal capability for the experience to keep compounding. The goal is for your organisation to run this without us.
