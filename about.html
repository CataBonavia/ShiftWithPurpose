What we work on

01

Customer & Employee Experience

How it feels to be a participant, client, or staff member from first contact to ongoing relationship. We design it deliberately, not by accident.

02

Service Design

Implementation Support

We stay alongside you until the work is embedded. Not consulting from a distance; designing the detail, supporting change, and building capability so the organisation runs it independently.

Catalina Bonavia

From Strategy to Making it Happen

Hi, I'm Catalina; an Argentine-born industrial designer and service strategist based in Melbourne.

In 2014, fresh out of university, I took a job at a start-up creating housing solutions for low-income families in Argentina. That project changed my life and my career. I spent months visiting families, drinking mate with them, learning about their motivations, needs and ways of thinking. The outcome was a bathroom that can be built in eight hours with no prior knowledge or trade skills. But what changed me wasn't the solution; it was the process to get there.

Three things happened on that project that I've carried into every engagement since.

  • First, discovering what it really means to start by understanding the challenge and the people at the heart of it.
  • Second, hearing "that's impossible" from experienced professionals and learning to look at problems with a creative mind that doesn't accept the first answer as the final one.
  • Third, finding a sense of purpose I've never been willing to leave behind.

After that I built my career at the intersection of human experience and systems change; building the Employee Experience team at RMIT and changing how HR approached their projects, co-designing and implementing a new business permits process across more than ten councils in Victoria, and redesigning and deploying an industry-leading supported employee experience with and for people with intellectual disabilities.

I founded SHiFT with Purpose because I kept watching the same pattern repeat: purpose-led organisations doing genuinely important work, with good people and real intent, whose participant, client and employee experiences were being held back by systems, sequences and structures that were never designed for them. A gap between purpose, design and implementation that nobody was quite bridging.

When I partner with an organisation, I work with everyone from the shop floor to the board room and beyond.

I'll be honest: I also founded SHiFT with Purpose to have more flexibility and do the work I love directly with the clients. I bring in other experts when their specific skills are needed, but I am directly and deeply involved in every engagement; from the first workshop to the final handover. Continuity of thinking is part of the design.

Moving countries teaches you to read unfamiliar environments quickly. To notice what others have stopped seeing. To ask the question the room has decided is too obvious, or too uncomfortable. That's the lens every engagement starts with.

I can't wait to help you SHiFT with Purpose.

How we work

The SHiFT Method

Four stages. One continuous thread. Everything connects.

SEE

Map the experience as it actually exists

Not the org chart version. We sit with frontline workers, interview participants and clients, and collaborate with leadership to build a picture that brings both worlds together. We ask the questions the room has stopped asking.

SHAPE

Design around behaviours and needs

Co-design workshops where your team builds the solution with us. We design for how humans actually behave; then layer in AI and other technology deliberately. The change is easier because your people designed it.

SHiFT

Implement so it doesn't stay on a slide deck

Change activity, communications, and hands-on support through implementation. We stay alongside you; not watching from the sideline, but making sure the design becomes the reality.

SUSTAIN

Build capability so it keeps improving

Track what changes, measure what matters, and build the internal capability for the experience to keep compounding. The goal is for your organisation to run this without us.
