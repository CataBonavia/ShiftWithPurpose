You’re booked in.

What if this call changes the course of your organisation? A CEO I work with calls me his secret weapon. You might have just found yours.

You don’t need to have it all figured out

Most leaders I speak with can’t quite name what’s going on; and that’s ok. We’ll figure it out together. Booking this call means you’re one step closer to a transformation that matters, and I’m genuinely excited to walk through this SHiFT with you.

What happens next

Over the next couple of days I’ll send you a few things; how I think, how I work, and some of the shifts I’ve helped organisations like yours make. I promise it won’t be a lengthy boring document; just enough context so that when we meet, you already know who you’re walking in to speak with.

The call itself is 30–45 minutes. Come as you are. The best conversations I have start with someone saying “I’m not entirely sure how to describe it, but something isn’t quite working.”

That’s exactly where we begin.

In the meantime

If something comes to mind before our call; a detail you forgot to add, something you want me to know, just reply to any of my emails. I read everything.

And if you’re anything like me, you’ll want something to chew on before we meet. So here’s a question I ask all my clients before we begin:

What would make you feel comfortable throughout our time together? And is there anything (positive or negative) from a past experience, with transformation, change, or consultants, that still sits with you?

Your answer to that is the foundation of how we build something that actually works.

I’ll see you soon.
