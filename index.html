Our signature methodology

We use our SHiFT Method® to find, build and launch what’s next for your organisation.

01 — SEE CLEARLY

See what’s really going on

Understand the people, organisation, context, constraints and opportunity.

02 — HARVEST OPPORTUNITIES

Find what’s already hiding inside your organisation

Uncover unmet needs, untapped assets and opportunities to create new value and owned revenue.

03 — INNOVATE INTENTIONALLY

Create something people want to buy

Monetise into a service, offer, experience or new way of creating value.

04 — FORGE MOMENTUM

05 — TAKE IT FORWARD

Test, learn and continue to grow your impact

We put the smallest useful version in front of real buyers; a paid pilot, pre-sale, or prototype designed to generate the strongest possible evidence.

Meet the Founder

I’m Catalina Bonavia, a strategist, service designer, creative problem-solver and professional dot-connector. I’m obsessed with people, possibilities and making good things happen.

For more than 14 years, I’ve worked in the purpose-led space; helping organisations turn big ambitions into services people actually want, business models that make sense and ideas that get out of the strategy deck and into the real world.

I’m a people-obsessed creative who also cares deeply about the numbers. I find the potential hiding in what you already have; your people, expertise, assets, relationships and reputation; and help you turn it into something valuable, sustainable and worth getting excited about.

I believe purpose-led organisations shouldn’t have to choose between doing good and making good money.

You can grow your impact, your income and your organisation; without growing away from what makes you, you. That’s values-led growth. And that’s what I’m here to help you create.
