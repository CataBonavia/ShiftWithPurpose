Is this right for you?

This is for the organisation that is ready to go deeper

You have already done the one-off work; the consultant came, the session happened, the report was written. And now you are wondering why it has not quite compounded the way you hoped. The pieces are good. They just are not connected yet.

A SHiFT Partnership is for leaders who understand that experience design is not a one-time project. Experience design is a strategic capability. We work with leaders who want someone alongside them; not advising from a distance, but doing the work, making decisions together, staying until it is embedded.

Sectors we work with

Disability services, aged care, health and allied health, community services, social enterprises, and purpose-led businesses; genuinely purpose-led, not pretend ones.

Typically starts with

A SHiFT Catalyst session to establish the baseline and design foundation. For Partnership clients, the Catalyst is included in the overall investment.

What's included

  • Monthly facilitation and design work; co-designing new experiences, processes, and service models
  • Leadership support and decision-making guidance from someone who understands your sector, your regulatory environment, and your team dynamics
  • AI capability building; practical, ethics-first adoption for the people who need it most
  • Metrics and momentum; regular check-ins, progress reviews, and iteration
  • Assets and tools design; the things your team reaches for every day to do their work well
  • Implementation support; staying alongside you through the gap between "we designed something excellent" and "our people are actually doing it differently"
The journey

The rhythm of a SHiFT Partnership

1

Phase 1

SEE: 1 to 6 months

Deep listening. We speak with your customers or participants, your team, your frontline workers, your leaders, and your partners. We map what is actually happening against what you want to be true. We identify the highest-leverage opportunities. The timeframe depends on the complexity and size of your organisation and the pace at which you can move.

2

Phase 2

SHAPE: typically 3 to 6 months

We design together. New experiences, new processes, new capability frameworks. We start with the moments that matter most and build outward. The scope depends on complexity, the number of initiatives, and your pace.

3

Phase 3

SHiFT and SUSTAIN

Implementation, iteration, and embedding. We stay alongside your team as the changes go live. We build their capability so the work continues long after the partnership does.
