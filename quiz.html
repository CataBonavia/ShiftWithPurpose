What it measures

Five layers of onboarding experience

The EAI measures each layer independently, so the result is precise — not general. You'll know exactly which one to strengthen first.

1

First contact and access

How easy is it for someone to find you, understand what you offer, and take the first step?

2

Welcome and belonging

Does the first interaction reflect your values? Do people feel welcomed or processed?

3

Information and clarity

Do people leave their early interactions knowing what to expect? Or do they have to chase?

4

Relationship and continuity

Is there consistency across staff and interactions? Does the experience depend on who shows up that day?

5

Integration and embedding

Does the person feel truly connected to the organisation after the first 30 days, or are they still warming up?
