Common questions

Before you book

Is this actually free, or is it a sales call?

It's genuinely free and genuinely not a sales call. You'll leave with something useful whether we work together or not. If there's a good fit and you want to go further, we'll talk about that; but only if you bring it up.

Who is this call for?

CEOs, COOs, directors, and founders of purpose-led NFPs, disability services organisations, aged care providers, or social enterprises. If you're the person who owns the challenge, this call is for you.

Do I need to prepare anything?

No. Just come with the thing that's been on your mind. The initiative that isn't compounding. The process that keeps coming back. The team that's carrying more than they should. That's enough.

What happens after the call?

Cata will send a short summary of what came up and what to focus on first. If a SHiFT Catalyst or Partnership makes sense, she'll mention it. If not, the summary is yours to use however you want.
