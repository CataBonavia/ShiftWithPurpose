I love talking with ambitious purpose-led leaders.

I help values-driven businesses, social enterprises and not-for-profit organisations grow by making their services easier to deliver, better to experience and more valuable to the people they serve.

How I can help you grow and stand out:

  • SHiFT Catalyst session: A focused strategic session to tackle one important challenge, find practical solutions and walk away with a clear implementation plan.
  • SHiFT Partnership: Ongoing strategic and executive-level support to help you design and implement better services, experiences and ways of working across your organisation.
  • The Revenue Lab: A four-month, 1:1 advisory engagement to uncover, design and develop new revenue streams or make existing ones work harder.

Not sure which is right for you? No drama! That's what our conversation is for. We'll explore what you're trying to achieve and work out the best way forward.

Purpose-led organisations shouldn't have to choose between impact and innovation.
