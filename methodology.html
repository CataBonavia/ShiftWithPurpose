How we work

The SHiFT Method

Four stages. One continuous thread. Everything connects — from the first research conversation to the moment your team runs the experience independently.

SEE

Map the experience as it actually exists

Not the org chart version. Not what leadership thinks is happening. We sit with frontline workers, interview participants and clients, and build a picture of what people actually experience from first contact through to ongoing service.

We collaborate with leadership and the board to understand and design a direction. We bring both worlds together into one picture.

We ask the questions the room has stopped asking, to uncover the possibilities that insiders are too close to see.

SHAPE

Design around behaviours and needs, not just process

Co-design workshops where your team builds the solution with us. We design for how humans actually behave; then layer in AI (and other technology) deliberately — automating repetitive tasks, surfacing insights, connecting systems that should already talk to each other.

The change is easier because your people designed it.

SHiFT

Implement so the work doesn't stay on a slide deck

We support you through implementation; designing the details, providing change and communications support. We stay alongside you and your team — not as a consultant watching from the sideline, but as a partner making sure the design becomes the reality.

Where required, AI tools are designed and your team is supported to adopt them — always specifically designed for your context and handed over with your people at the controls.

SUSTAIN

Build capability so it keeps improving

Track what changes, measure what matters, and build the internal capability for the experience to keep compounding.

The goal is for your organisation to run this without us — because the best outcome is that you no longer need us for your day-to-day.

Framework

The Experience Architecture Framework

Experience doesn't break in one place. It leaks across five layers, and most organisations are focused on the wrong one.

The Experience Architecture Framework maps the five layers where participant and employee loyalty is built or lost — and gives you a way to diagnose which layer is where your organisation's experience is actually leaking.

Most teams focus on touchpoints; the form, the conversation, the onboarding video. The EAF goes deeper — into the systems, sequences, culture, and capability that hold the touchpoints together. Or don't.

Layer 1 — First contact & onboarding

How it feels to come in for the first time. The quality of the welcome, the clarity of information, the speed of resolution.

Layer 2 — Ongoing touchpoints

Whether the experience is deliberate and consistent, or dependent on who picks up the phone that day.

Layer 3 — Systems & tools

Whether your technology supports or fights your team's ability to deliver well. The systems that should talk to each other. The ones that don't.

Layer 4 — Culture & capability

Whether your team has the skills, clarity, and confidence to deliver the experience that's been designed — or whether good intentions keep getting filtered through individuals.

Layer 5 — Leadership & strategy alignment

Whether leadership's stated direction and the lived experience of participants and staff are pointing in the same direction. The gap between the deck and the door.

AI approach

Think Then AI

AI should amplify purpose, not replace the people behind it.

Before we recommend a single tool, we understand the humans. Your clients, your staff, your community. We don't open with a technology recommendation; we arrive at it after the human picture is clear.

In purpose-led organisations, the sequence matters more than in most sectors. The people you serve are often in vulnerable circumstances. The staff delivering your services carry real emotional weight. Introducing AI without understanding that context first isn't innovation; it's risk.

This isn't anti-AI. It's pro-human. The two things are not in conflict — and in purpose-led organisations, getting that sequence right is everything.

Human-centred before AI

We understand your people and processes first. Technology comes in at the end of that sequence — deliberately, not as the starting point.

Platform-agnostic

We don't sell software. We help you choose the right tools for your team, your context, and your budget — and build the skills to use them independently.

Ethical by design

Nothing gets automated without deliberate consideration. In regulated sectors like disability, aged care, and health, that's not optional — it's the standard.

Built with your team, not for them

AI tools that work are the ones your people helped design and feel confident using. We build them together and hand them over with training.
