The process

The architecture of the day

This is a strategic intensive built like an iceberg; most of the value is in what happens before and after the day itself.

1 to 3 weeks before

Pre-session

  • — Review of your current customer or employee experience materials
  • — Pre-session form completed by your team so the agenda is built for your situation
  • — Approximately eight hours of design preparation
  • — 1:1 meeting with the CEO and up to two other leaders

The working session

Half-day, three to four hours

  • — Up to five C-level leaders in the room
  • — Mapping the experience as it actually exists, not as it appears on the org chart
  • — Designing one signature experience, end-to-end
  • — AI opportunity mapping using the Think Then AI methodology
  • — Building the 90-day plan together, so your team owns it

The fortnight after

Post-session

  • — All deliverables designed and delivered as working documents
  • — 60-minute check-in two to four weeks later, locked in your calendar before we close
Deliverables

What you walk away with

Five named artefacts. Designed by us, owned by you, ready to act on.

01

Customer Journey Map

Designed and delivered as a working document your team can use on Monday; not filed away and forgotten.

02

One Signature Experience

Designed end-to-end; the door your participants or staff walk through, built to reflect your values at every touchpoint.

03

AI Opportunity Map

Built with the Think Then AI methodology: where AI amplifies your team, where it doesn't, and where it's a clear no.

04

90-Day Now / Next / Later Plan

Named owners and metrics. Not a wish list; a plan your team built and actually owns.

05

60-Minute Check-in

Two to four weeks post-session, locked in your calendar before the day ends. Because implementation always surfaces questions.

The methodology underneath

Think Then AI

In purpose-led sectors, AI shouldn't replace the people doing good. It should multiply their capacity to do it. Think Then AI is the methodology that runs underneath every decision in the day.

Think.

Understand the people, the process, and the purpose before any technology touches them. Map what's actually working. Find what's almost working but not quite landing.

Then.

Design the experience deliberately; build coherence between marketing, intake, delivery, and renewal. Make your values visible at every touchpoint.

AI.

Choose where AI amplifies your team and where it doesn't belong. Ethical, human-first, inside your real-world constraints.

Why us

From Strategy to Making it Happen

I'm Catalina; an Argentine-born industrial designer and service strategist based in Melbourne. My career has been at the intersection of human experience and systems change; from designing a bathroom that can be built in eight hours with no prior knowledge, to building the Employee Experience team at RMIT University, to co-designing service systems across more than ten councils in Victoria.

I founded SHiFT with Purpose because I kept watching the same pattern repeat: purpose-led organisations with good people and real intent, whose participant, client and employee experiences were being held back by systems and structures that were never designed for them.
