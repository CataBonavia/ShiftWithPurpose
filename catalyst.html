Is this right for you?

This is for you if…

You have been doing the work; the training, the restructure, the new system; and something still is not adding up. The pieces are there, but they are not connecting into something coherent. The experience your participants or clients have depends on who they speak to, not on a deliberate design.

Or you have a growth moment coming; a funding shift, a new contract, a merger, a service expansion; and you need to make sure the experience is built for what is next, not patched together from what is current.

Or you simply need a day with someone who understands your sector, understands experience design, and can help you see clearly what is actually going on.

What you walk away with

  • Customer journey map reflecting your real current state
  • One signature experience designed; typically onboarding, intake, or a key service moment
  • AI opportunity map: where to use AI, where not to, built around your actual workflows using the Think Then AI methodology
  • A 90-day Now / Next / Later plan with owners and metrics
  • A 60-minute check-in session booked 2–4 weeks later

Half-day or full day options available, in-person or online. Cata reviews your current state before the session. You do not need to spend time getting her up to speed on the day.

Not a fit if: You are looking just for a report to present to the board and do nothing with it later. The SHiFT Catalyst is a working session, not a deliverable to file away.

The process

What a SHiFT Catalyst looks like

Before the session

Thorough preparation

Cata reviews your current state; your customer journey, your onboarding process, your recent feedback, your team structure. She comes prepared. You do not need to spend time getting her up to speed on the day.

During the session

Collaborative and holistic work

We map where things are, honestly and specifically. We design where they need to be, starting with the moments that matter most to your customers, partners, and team. We build a 90-day plan with clear owners and metrics so the work continues after the day is done.

After the session

Supported follow-through

The follow-up session is built in because implementation always surfaces questions. It is not optional. Some people take it from here after the Catalyst; for others, it is the start of a SHiFT journey together. Both are valid, both work.

The method

Why this SHiFT sticks

Most facilitated strategy sessions produce momentum that lasts about a week. The SHiFT Catalyst is structured differently.

We do not start with solutions, ever. We start with understanding; what is actually happening, for the people experiencing it. That means the design we produce is grounded in your real context, not imported from somewhere else.

The 90-day plan is not a wish list. It has owners, metrics, and a clear first step. And the follow-up session means you have someone to call when the implementation gets complicated.
