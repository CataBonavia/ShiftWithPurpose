Our approach

What makes SHiFT different

We take you from idea to bringing it to life. We bring the creativity to solve complex problems and the systems brain to make it happen; the empathy to really understand your context and people, and the engagement to bring them on the journey.

Think Designer, Programme Management, Implementation and Change Champion; in one pocket.

We build on what you have already done.

Your prior investment; the CRM, the brand refresh, the process mapping, the training; is the foundation we design from. The goal is to connect what exists into something that compounds.

You are a collaborator, not just a client.

We create this together. We are not going to bring you a solution that was previously designed for someone else, because it won't work. We co-design, implement alongside you, and stay until it is embedded in how your organisation actually works.

Creativity meets compliance.

Your world is complex or highly regulated? Those are the problems we love the most. If a solution cannot be delivered by your team within your regulatory environment, it has failed. If it looks the same as everyone else's, it has failed too. Nothing of that exists in SHiFT.

You work with our founder, start to finish.

No junior staff doing the work, no handover to an implementation team. No cookie-cutter frameworks applied by someone who has never worked in your sector. You get Cata; from the first conversation through to the last check-in. And yes, we may bring in some experts where we need them, but Cata is still your person.

Still figuring out where to begin?

That is exactly what the Discovery Call is for. Thirty minutes to know each other, see if we are a good fit, understand what's in your mind and to-do list and work out what is most useful for you right now.
